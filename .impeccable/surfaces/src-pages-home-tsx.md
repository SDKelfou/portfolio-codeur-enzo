## Direction contract

THESIS: A living interface: digital porcelain that cracks open to reveal its other side. The theme switch is the signature: the screen fractures from the button and the new theme shows through the cracks. Refuses the dark-neon dev portfolio, the SaaS card grid and the 3D-model hero.

OWN-WORLD: Porcelain (#F8F6F0 ground, #FFFFFF surfaces, ink #24222D, lavender #DCD0F5 / iris #A99BCF tints) and Obsidian (#101016, night #191B2B, graphite #272432, lavender #C4B1F5, soft cyan #9ADDDC secondary). One light: yellow #FFF010, used as the fracture's inner light, the primary action fill (ink text on it) and focus. Instrument Serif giant editorial display (italic for the name), Manrope for text. Hairline cracks as section joins; faceted shard sculpture in SVG.

STORY: The visitor reads "Enzo, développeur web" at giant scale, sees what he builds and from 799 €, flips the theme and watches the page crack, scrolls through real work revealed behind masks, clear pricing, the person, then writes a quote email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Asymmetric: giant "Enzo" in italic serif spanning ~70% width on the left, one-line activity and short intro beneath, yellow "Demander un devis" + link "Voir les réalisations"; right: the shard sculpture assembling with particles; bottom edge: small technical details (live load time, languages, response delay) and a scroll invitation. Nav with a distinct theme-switch button (the fracture origin).

FORM: FRACTURE (user-pinned brief; overrides the rolled Chronométrage, seed 2b1445f6, whose live-timing readout survives as a technical detail). Signature interaction: Reality Fracture theme switch via View Transitions with a jagged clip-path reveal plus crack lines and shards. Motion grammar: Genesis assembly 900 ms, mask reveals on scroll, page transitions as a diagonal crack wipe 450 ms, everything visible by default and reduced-motion safe.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
